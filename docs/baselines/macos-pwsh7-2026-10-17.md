## The 12 known platform differences

In each, otterPS on PowerShell 7 behaves differently from otterPS on Windows
PowerShell 5.1, and otter-net gives the 5.1 behaviour (the Windows CI
comparison shows 0 differences for these programs). They are **documented
compatibility differences, not classified as harmless**: what Otter should
do on every platform is an open specification question, tracked in
[the cross-platform consistency proposal](../CROSS-PLATFORM-CONSISTENCY.md).
otter-net's behaviour stays as it is until that is decided.

A local run that shows any difference not in this list is a regression.
